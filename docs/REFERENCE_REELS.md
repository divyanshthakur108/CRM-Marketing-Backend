## 3. What makes it look "neat and clean" (the style rules)
1. **The presenter is the spine.** She appears in ~60% of the reel, in 5–6 locations (garden, sofa, window, hedges, office, sales lounge), same outfit, so it feels like one continuous talk. Renders are cut-aways, never more than ~6 s.
2. **One look throughout:** shallow depth of field, soft vignette, warm skin and slightly teal shadows (consistent grade), clean bright exposure.
3. **Typography system (only two styles):**
   - Headline: large serif italic (Playfair/Didone-like), white, no box, with soft shadow: "Idhar Aaiye!", "Sector 91", "Premium", "5.37 Acres", "Elevated Walkway", "100%".
   - Detail: small bold uppercase sans in **white rounded pills with black text**, or plain white caps under the headline: "QUARK CITY", "5 PREMIUM TOWERS", "LAND REGISTRATION".
   - Placed in the lower-middle (about 60–80% height), never over the face; pills pop in one by one.
4. **One message per shot, 2–5 words.** Numbers are the hero ("5.37 Acres", "100%", "3+1 & 4+1").
5. **Graphics composited into the scene:** satellite map card on the wall with a glowing highlighted plot; amenity photo cards fanning out behind/above the presenter. These explain location and amenities without long text.
6. **Hook in the first 2 s:** gesture + "Idhar Aaiye!" (Hinglish) + "important announcement" pill.
7. **Transitions:** blur-in, light leaks / flares, gentle push-ins. No hard flashy effects.
8. **Branding:** project logo early (3–6 s) and at the end, developer logo end card on black.
9. **Story order:** hook → project name → location (map + nearby hubs) → premium positioning → land size / towers → configurations → interiors → unique USP (elevated walkway) → amenities → trust (100% land registration) → lifestyle → logo.
10. Sound: not checked (see the note at the top). The text ("Idhar Aaiye! Ek important announcement hai") suggests a Hinglish presenter voice; confirm by listening.

## 3a. How each caption animates (close look)

The captions are **synced to what the presenter says**: words appear the moment she says them, and the text stays on screen until the next idea.

| Element | How it comes in | How it goes out | Where seen |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Serif italic headline** ("Idhar Aaiye!", "Sector 91", "Premium", "Elevated Walkway") | **Word by word**, in time with speech. Each word rises a few pixels and settles (at 1.2 s "Aaiye!" is still lower and smaller than "Idhar"; by 1.4 s they're on one line). "Sector" appears first, "91" a beat later | **Glow/bloom out**: the text turns bright white, blurs and spreads like light, then vanishes (6.3 s, 55.6 s, 68 s, 73.2 s) | Throughout |
| **Pill (white rounded box, black uppercase text)** | Starts as a **small white circle**, then **stretches sideways while the words type in one by one** as she says them: "EK" → "EK IMPORTANT" → "… ANNOUNCEMENT" → "… ANNOUNCEMENT HAI" (2.2 → 2.85 s). Short pills ("QUARK CITY") just pop in | Same glow/bloom out, or a **glitch smear** (horizontal RGB streaks, 3.0 s) on a shot change | 2–3 s, 9–13 s, 25–28 s, 71–72 s, 76 s |
| **Pill groups** ("QUARK CITY", "IT HUBS", "PHARMA UNITS", "INDUSTRIAL AREA") | One pill at a time, ~1 s apart, as each place is named; they **wrap into centred rows** (2 per row), with equal gaps. Earlier pills don't move | All together with the headline | 8.8–13 s |
| **Sub-line in plain white caps** ("RESIDENTIAL PROJECT", "LAND REGISTRATION", "APPROVALS", "FIRST OF ITS KIND") | Word by word, each word rising into place (at 14.7 s "PROJECT" is still higher than "RESIDENTIAL") | With its headline | 14–17 s, 40–44 s, 65–69 s |
| **Big numbers** ("100%") | **Slide in from the left** (at 65.0 s it's half off-screen), settle in the centre | Bloom out | 65 s |
| **Headline that stays** ("5.37 Acres") | Fades in once | Stays fixed **across 4 different render shots**, so the cuts feel connected | 22–31 s |
| **Logo** (REGAL Empirus) | Motion-blur **whip/zoom** in (4.0–4.3 s, 73.8 s) | Cut | 4–7 s, 74–77 s |
| **Amenity word** ("Pickleball" → "Premium Clubhouse" → "Indoor Games" → "Party Hall") | Changes with each card | Bloom out, next word appears in the same spot | 52–64 s |

Other details that make the text look clean:
- **Placement changes with the picture:** over the presenter, text sits at about 65–80% of the height (on her body, under the face). Over the render fly-through, where the bottom is busy, text moves to the **top** ("5.37 Acres" + pills at 10–30%). Logo lock-up sits mid-frame.
- **A dark gradient at the bottom** of most shots so white text reads without any box.
- **Headline over pill/sub-line**, always centred, always the same two fonts, same sizes all reel long.
- **Mix of Hindi-in-English letters and English:** "Idhar Aaiye!", "EK IMPORTANT ANNOUNCEMENT HAI" (Hinglish hook), the rest in English.
- **Shot transitions:** warm orange light-leak flash (3.5 s, 13.8 s), white flash (64.6 s), glitch smear (3.0 s), motion-blur whip (73.8 s).

# Part C: combined style guide (what all 5 share)

1. **Two fonts only:** bold sans (white) for normal words + **serif italic** (white or gold) for the key word or number. Pink/gold/white brand colours, no other colours.
2. **Text follows the voice:** either full word-by-word subtitles with one gold key word (R5) or short headline + pill (R1). Our current "lux" captions do neither.
3. **One idea per screen, numbers as heroes:** 5.37 ACRES, 14 ACRES, 9 Acres / 8 Towers / 600 Families, 100%, 205 A, 2 km.
4. **Text never covers the face:** bottom-middle over the presenter, top over busy shots.
5. **Presenter on site** carries the reel; renders/drone/stock are short cut-aways.
6. **Location as a graphic:** designed map with pin and distance chips (R5) or a glowing plot outline on a drone/aerial shot (R3, R4).
7. **Trust beats:** "100% registered land", "80/90% approvals", showing the real logo wall, hoarding and land (R2).
8. **Brand:** small developer logo in a corner throughout (R5), project logos on fact screens, logo whip-in end card.
9. **Clean transitions:** white flash, light leak, motion-blur whip, blur-in. Nothing else.
10. **Length:** 26–50 s for single-message reels, 80–118 s for full project tours.
